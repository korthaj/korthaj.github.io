About YourBasic

yourbasic.org

YourBasic is an educational website and open-source project focused on algorithms and the Go program­ming language. It was founded in April 2018 by Stefan Nilsson.

Free AI Guide for Everyone [även på svenska]

The university has three tasks: education, research and spreading knowledge to everyone. My contribution is a freely available book in English and Swedish on how to use AI safely and effectively, and to understand its strengths and limitations. No prerequisites.
